Step 1 — Wire the circuit yourself

📋 Wiring guidelines — read first, then connect manually

  1. This is a series circuit: every component connects one after another in a single loop, with no branches.
  2. Connect the battery's + terminal to either terminal of the resistor.
  3. Connect the resistor's other terminal to either terminal of the ammeter.
  4. Connect the ammeter's remaining terminal back to the battery's − terminal to close the loop.
  5. Each terminal only takes one wire — wiring two terminals of the same component together will short it out and be rejected.

Drag from one terminal (the small circles) to another to draw a wire — or tap one terminal, then tap the terminal you want to join it to.

Ohm's Law states that the current flowing through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points, provided temperature and other physical conditions remain constant.

V = I × R

Where V is voltage in volts, I is current in amperes, and R is resistance in ohms.

Aim: To verify Ohm's Law and determine the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance by wiring and simulating a series circuit.

Virtual apparatus: DC battery source, carbon-film resistor, digital ammeter, connecting wires — dragged into place and rendered as manipulable 3D objects.
