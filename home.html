How the virtual lab works

Same flow every practical follows, so it stays easy to navigate.

  1. Register and log in to unlock your personal dashboard.
  2. Pick an experiment card — each opens a dedicated 3D workspace.
  3. Adjust parameters with sliders; the 3D model and readouts update live.
  4. Rotate, zoom, and pan the model to inspect it from any angle.
  5. Follow the step-by-step procedure panel to complete the practical.
  6. Rate the experiment — your feedback feeds the usability analytics page.
